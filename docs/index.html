The band

New songs.
Played like the room depends on it.

Three Dublin musicians making new original material together: short, melodic, high-energy punk and guitar pop. Their histories run back through the city’s punk scene, including The Strougers, The End, The Lee Harveys, Trouble Pilgrims, Clash Jam Wallop and more. The Deviators aren’t interested in recreating any of it.
