# The Deviators — Website Improvement Plan

Planning date: 19 September 2026  
Status: Draft for review; implementation has not started.

## Purpose

Make thedeviators.com a living electronic press kit: a current, distinctive website that lets a promoter, journalist or new listener understand the band, hear or see a performance, find the next gig and contact management quickly.

The first screen should communicate who The Deviators are and offer an immediate way to watch or listen. The site should support gig attendance, press coverage and booking enquiries.

## Basis for this plan

- Review of the live Home, Music, Meet the Band and Get in Touch pages.
- Website recommendations retrieved from the task “Management Plan Review”.
- The two-page `The_Deviators_EPK.pdf` supplied in this project folder.
- Nine supplied photographs, including `Hero shot.jpg` and `in rehearsal.jpg`.

The live-site review identified excessive empty header space, no obvious listening option on the Music page, limited introductory text on Meet the Band, and a dated gig item in the main navigation. Mobile behaviour, performance, form delivery and all external links still need testing.

## 1. Site structure

Use five main pages: **Home · Live · About · Media · Press / EPK**.

Keep a prominent **Book the Band** link in the header and management contact and social links in every footer. Music should be accessible directly from the homepage and Press / EPK page. A separate Music page can be added later when the release catalogue warrants it.

### Home

Build the page in this order:

1. **Hero:** band logo or clear wordmark, strong live photograph, one-line introduction, and Listen / Watch Live actions. Working positioning: “Original Dublin punk and guitar pop.” Treat this as draft copy.
2. **Next gig:** full date and year, venue, city, time, ticket price and direct ticket link. For the current Grand Social announcement, distinguish the advertised start time from doors time if both are available.
3. **Featured performance or music:** one strong video and, where authorised for public use, a track or release link. Give visitors a useful experience without requiring social-media navigation.
4. **Band introduction:** a concise paragraph adapted from the EPK, introducing Bitzy Fitzgerald, Bren Lynott and Andy and the current band's sound.
5. **Press quote:** one short, accurately attributed quotation, with a source link where available.
6. **Album panel:** *More Volume, Less Reverb*, Stano production credit, approved artwork when available, and accurate release status. Feature *Tokyo* when its release information and destination links are confirmed.
7. **Booking and press:** direct management contact plus a link to Press / EPK.

Select the hero crop after reviewing all supplied photos. The supplied hero photograph is portrait-oriented: test its desktop and mobile crops before committing to a full-width treatment.

### Live

- List upcoming confirmed shows chronologically, each with date, venue, location and ticket/details link.
- Highlight the next show without duplicating a long announcement.
- Move completed shows to a short selected-history section.
- Include the Grand Social performance video where useful.
- Replace the dated “Live @TGS 31st May 2026” navigation item with the permanent Live page.
- If there are no upcoming dates, show a clear message and booking contact.

### About

- Start with the current band: sound, songs and live identity.
- Follow with a concise band story and a photograph of all three members.
- Add short individual biographies for Bitzy, Bren and Andy, adapted from the EPK.
- Confirm member display names and instruments before final copy approval; do not infer missing details from previous bands.
- Keep previous-band history concise and secondary.
- Include Bitzy's book as a discreet related link.

### Media

- Begin with the best of the nine supplied photographs. Aim for 8–12 strong images only if enough suitable material is available.
- Separate press portraits and live photography where it helps visitors find a suitable image.
- Supply photographer credits and clear download labels.
- Use original high-resolution files for press downloads. Check supplied image dimensions before describing anything as high resolution.
- Offer logo files when supplied, ideally with transparent background and light/dark versions.
- Use descriptive captions and image alternative text; avoid generic labels such as “image1”.

### Press / EPK

- Present a short pitch, copyable short and long biographies, selected press quotes and management contact.
- Provide a current downloadable PDF EPK suitable for public distribution.
- Link to approved listening material, live video, press photographs and logos.
- Credit Stano on the album information.
- Include The Goo and Colm O'Hare quotations from the supplied EPK with accurate attribution. Retrieve the exact Ferdia Mac Anna quotation and its source before adding it.
- Make key information available on the page so a visitor does not have to download a PDF to understand the band.

## 2. Visual direction

- Mostly monochrome, with large live photographs and a restrained accent colour. Burgundy is an option to test, not a locked decision.
- Bold, readable headings and straightforward body typography.
- Use the distressed band logo sparingly; keep body text and controls clean.
- Reduce the large empty area above the current navigation/content.
- Use consistent spacing, button styles, photo treatment and capitalisation.
- Keep motion minimal and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Design for phone screens from the start, especially hero cropping, gig details and booking links.

## 4. Delivery sequence

### Phase 1 — Content preparation

Produce a content inventory, draft page copy, selected photo set and a short list of unresolved facts. Establish the primary management contact and identify public media links. Inspect the existing GoDaddy editing access and capabilities before deciding how to implement the design; this plan does not assume a platform migration.

**Complete when:** every planned section has usable draft content or a clearly identified missing asset. Public and private materials are distinguished.

### Phase 2 — Homepage design

Create desktop and mobile homepage mockups using the real band photography and EPK copy. Establish typography, colours, navigation, buttons and photo treatment. Check that the opening view introduces the band and offers an immediate next action.

**Complete when:** the homepage layout, content order and visual direction have been reviewed and agreed.

### Phase 3 — Website implementation

Build the five pages using the agreed design. Add gig links, public music/video, contact options, media downloads and the public EPK. Keep the existing domain. Preserve useful existing URLs or redirect them to the relevant new pages; map Music, Meet the Band, Gallery and Get in Touch deliberately, including the old live-section anchor where feasible.

**Complete when:** all five pages work in a reviewable preview, navigation is consistent and no placeholder text or unapproved private links remain.

### Phase 4 — Checks and launch preparation

- Review narrow mobile, tablet and desktop layouts for clipping, readable text and image crops.
- Verify keyboard navigation, visible focus, heading order, image alternative text and text contrast.
- Compress display images, load below-the-fold media only when needed, and avoid autoplay audio/video.
- Verify every ticket, social, email, media and download link.
- Verify contact-form validation and arrange an end-to-end delivery check with the intended recipient.
- Give every page a descriptive title and search description; set a suitable social-sharing image.
- Check cookie-choice behaviour and retain relevant privacy information.
- Test redirects from existing pages and the not-found page.
- Check all gig/release information against the final supplied details.

**Complete when:** the reviewable site passes these checks and the remaining content decisions are resolved. Agree publishing access and the launch step once the finished preview is ready.

### Phase 5 — Maintenance

- Update dates as shows are confirmed and move completed shows out of the upcoming list.
- Update release panels when music becomes available.
- Refresh the EPK when a release, contact or major booking changes; show its revision date.
- Review public links and downloads monthly and before press campaigns.
- Keep a simple record of ticket clicks, listening/video clicks, EPK downloads and booking enquiries if suitable measurement is enabled.

## 5. First-version scope

Deliver five responsive pages, a consistent visual system, public listening/viewing access, a current gig list, selected credited media, a public-ready EPK and clear booking contact.

Defer a shop, news/blog system, extensive gig archive, mailing-list integration and elaborate animation until there is a clear need and someone to maintain them.

## 6. Immediate next step

Prepare the page copy and photo shortlist, then create the homepage mockup. The first review should settle the hero image, short band introduction, primary action, colour treatment and album/gig prominence. Resolve missing links and publication decisions alongside that work.
